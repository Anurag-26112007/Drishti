How it works

1

See

Camera frames go to an object detection model (COCO-SSD) running in your browser.

2

Understand

Box position gives the direction. Box size gives an approximate distance. A custom model I trained reads currency notes.

3

Speak

Short spoken sentences and large captions, in English or Hindi, with movement guidance such as "move slightly right".

The problem

Moving through unfamiliar places is hard without sight. A white cane detects what it touches, but not what is a few steps ahead or what an object is. Telling one currency note from another is also difficult.

Existing solutions

Apps such as Be My Eyes, Microsoft Seeing AI and Google Lookout help blind users. Many need an app install, several rely on an internet connection or a volunteer, and Hindi support is often limited.

What Drishti adds

  • Opens in any browser with no install.
  • Detection runs on the device, so the camera feed is not uploaded.
  • Says the object, direction and approximate distance.
  • Gives movement guidance and warns about close obstacles.
  • Reads Indian currency notes with a custom Teachable Machine model.
  • English and Hindi speech, large high-contrast captions.

Safety: Drishti is an assistive prototype. Distances are estimates and objects can be missed. It is not a replacement for a white cane, a guide dog or a human guide.
